People looking for a clinic often compare several before choosing. The landing page is frequently the first contact, and it has to answer fast the questions a patient has: *what do you do, who will see me, what is the first visit like, and how do I book?*

Here is what a good clinic landing page needs.

## 1. A clear promise at the top

On the first screen, say **what the clinic does and in which city**. "Aesthetic clinic in Campina Grande" says more than a generic slogan, helps the patient recognize themselves and helps Google understand the page.

## 2. Treatments explained simply

List the main procedures and explain each in a few lines: who it is for, how it works and what to expect. Avoid jargon. If there are points of attention, such as sensitivity or the need for a prior evaluation, say so honestly. It builds more trust than exaggerated promises.

## 3. Who will see the patient

People hire people. Show the professional with a good photo, training and registration number. A short text about how they work helps the patient picture the visit.

## 4. What the first visit is like

Many people don't book out of uncertainty. Explain in three or four steps what happens at the first visit, how long it takes and what to bring.

## 5. Social proof, carefully

Testimonials and results help, but **health advertising has rules**. Each professional council defines what can be published, including before-and-after images. Check your council's rules before publishing.

## 6. A WhatsApp button everywhere

The shortest path between a question and an appointment is a conversation. Place WhatsApp buttons with a pre-filled message at the top, middle and end of the page, plus a sticky button on mobile.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

A Q&A section handles objections (price, pain, recovery time, payment) and also helps on Google, because it answers exactly what people search for.

## 8. Speed and mobile first

Most patients arrive on a phone. If the page is slow, the visitor goes back to search and picks another clinic. Optimized images, few fonts and lean code make a difference.
