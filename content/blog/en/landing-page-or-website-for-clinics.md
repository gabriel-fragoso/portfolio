"Do I need a website or a landing page?" It's one of the most common questions from clinic owners who want to attract patients online. The answer depends on where the business is.

## Landing page

It is **a single page** with one goal: lead the visitor to an action, such as booking an appointment through WhatsApp. It presents the clinic, the treatments, who sees patients and the most common questions, without distractions.

**Pros**
- Faster to build and maintain.
- Focused: the visitor knows what to do.
- Works well for ads and local searches.

**Limits**
- Little room for many treatments or professionals.
- Fewer pages for Google to index.

## Full website

A **multi-page** site: home, team, one page per treatment, blog, contact.

**Pros**
- More room to explain each treatment in depth.
- More pages to show up in different searches.
- A more complete image.

**Limits**
- More work to build and keep updated.
- Needs quality content on every page.

## How to decide

- **Just starting, few treatments, or testing interest?** Start with a landing page.
- **Large team, many treatments, or investing in content?** A multi-page site with a blog is worth it.
- **Running ads?** A landing page is the natural choice, since each ad can lead to a focused page.

## They don't compete

A landing page can be the first step and grow into a full website later, reusing what was already built. Whichever you choose, the page should be **fast on mobile, clear, and with an obvious way to book**.
